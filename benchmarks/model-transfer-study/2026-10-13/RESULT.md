# Model-transfer study result

Conclusion: **inconclusive**.

The pilot stopped under the specified metering rule. A source-dependency agent
reached 600 seconds. The CLI supplied no token count for its last call. The
complete task/method matrix is unavailable. No token-saving estimate is valid.

Eight of 48 planned trajectories started. The record contains all completed
stages and the interrupted attempts. Four baseline trajectories passed their
final checks. No new-memory trajectory finished all three stages. Research,
the final written-rule comparison, and confirmation did not run.

One accepted source model passed its two saved cases. On 27 unseen inputs, it
gave 16 correct predictions and 11 wrong predictions. Its code deliverable
passed. This is diagnostic evidence of model overfit. It is not an independent
estimate of added task failures.

The study recorded at least 3,262,415 native tokens in the scored attempts.
The full total is unknown. Setup and abandoned development runs have separate
records. Cached tokens remain part of native input totals. No billing data was
used, and no money-saving claim is made.

Model: `gpt-5.6-sol`. Reasoning: `high`. Limits: 600 seconds and 40 study tool
calls per stage. Initial memory: at most 1,200 characters. The model-access,
metering, and isolation checks passed before workers started. Correct reference
programs passed all 24 stage checks. Both incorrect controls were rejected.
The task packs, source hashes, settings, and run order were frozen first.

The revised model instructions require local boundary cases and retained
counterexamples. They require restricted rules when observations do not support
generality. No new scored result is claimed for this revision.

The revised runner prevents a final call with less than 60 seconds left. It
stops peer dispatch on a metering fault. It saves completed trajectories at
once. All 32 harness tests pass. These repairs are unscored engineering checks.
The stopped pilot used the earlier frozen source. Its source archive is intact.

Files:

- `pilot-v3/manifest.json`: frozen settings, task hashes, order, and analysis.
- `pilot-v3/records.jsonl`: completed and interrupted trajectory records.
- `pilot-v3/runs/`: complete prompts, native events, tool results, memory, Git, and outputs.
- `pilot-v3/report.json`: machine-readable conclusion and diagnostic records.
- `pilot-v3/prediction-audit.json`: unseen model predictions.
- `pilot-v3/frozen-source-verification.json`: checks for the 60 archived source files.
- `setup-resources.json`: setup costs, including unknown counts.
- `study-tests-after-stop.xml`: the revised harness checks.

The saved tasks are constructed work samples from eight families. Their author
also wrote the oracle. Research sources are constructed records. This study
cannot establish performance on independent real-world projects.
